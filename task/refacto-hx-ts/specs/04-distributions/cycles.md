# Distribution cycles (DIS-CYC)

Actors: coordinator of all catalogs.

- **DIS-CYC-001** As a coordinator of all catalogs, I can create a cycle of distribution sessions that repeats weekly, every two weeks, every three weeks or monthly. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md S3.1, R18-R19; camap-hx/src/service/DistributionService.hx:752-790 -->

- **DIS-CYC-002** As a coordinator of all catalogs, creating a cycle generates all its sessions at once, up to 100. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md R19; camap-hx/src/service/DistributionService.hx:664-724 -->
  - Generation runs from the start date until the end date, which counts to the end of that day.
  - A monthly cycle keeps the same weekday and the same position in the month as the first date, falling back one week earlier when the month lacks it.

- **DIS-CYC-003** As a coordinator of all catalogs, I set for a cycle the start and end times, the number of days before the distribution when ordering opens and closes, and the opening and closing hours. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md R20; camap-hx/src/service/DistributionService.hx:637-658 -->
  - Both day counts are required.
  - Defaults: opening 10 days before at 08:00, closing 2 days before at 23:55, distribution 19:00 to 20:00.

- **DIS-CYC-004** As a coordinator of all catalogs, creating a cycle stops at the first date that conflicts with an existing session at the same place. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md R19; camap-hx/src/service/DistributionService.hx:664-724 -->
  - The sessions already created before the conflict stay; the later dates are not created.
  - There is no per-occurrence handling and the cycle validation stays commented out. This is intended.
  - **DECISION (UND-063, 2026-11-06)** Keep the current behaviour: a conflict aborts the generation, without skipping or reporting individual dates.

- **DIS-CYC-005** As a coordinator of all catalogs, sessions generated by a cycle start with no catalog participating. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md R18; camap-hx/src/controller/Distribution.hx:841 -->
  - I add catalogs per session or per catalog.

- **DIS-CYC-006** As a coordinator of all catalogs, I can delete a cycle. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md S3.1, R21; camap-hx/src/service/DistributionService.hx:730-747 -->
  - Each session of the cycle is deleted with the session rules, and errors are reported as messages.
  - The cycle is deleted afterwards.
  - Deletion needs no security token.

- **DIS-CYC-007** As a coordinator of all catalogs, the cycle list shows cycles whose dates are inside or span the shown period. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md R22; camap-hx/src/db/DistributionCycle.hx:43-52 -->

- **DIS-CYC-008** As a coordinator of all catalogs, the cycle edit, cycle insert and invite-vendors-to-cycle functions are unfinished and offer nothing yet. <!-- 03-features/distributions-volunteers.md S3.1; camap-hx/src/controller/Distribution.hx:399,549,684 -->
  - They remain empty or "coming soon" stubs, and the invite-to-cycle link stays disabled (`camap-hx/src/controller/Distribution.hx:399,549,684`, `camap-hx/lang/master/tpl/distribution/default.mtt:415-419`). This is intended.
  - **DECISION (UND-063, 2026-11-06)** Keep the current behaviour: the stubs are left as they are.
